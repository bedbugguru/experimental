Double Scoop

                

                
                    
                    Listen on Bandcamp
                
                
The debut album from bed bug guru. It was recorded live-to-tape over four days at The Atomic Garden in Oakland, California, by engineer Jack Shirley. Released November 7, 2025, on Superbloom Records. It includes early songs alongside new studio material. Video by Jake Faiella playing in the background.
